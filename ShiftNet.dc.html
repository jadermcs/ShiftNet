About & sources

ShiftNet adds dates to Open English WordNet. Each sense carries the date it was first recorded and a link to the sense it grew out of, so a word’s entry reads as a history as well as a list of meanings.

Data sources

Synsets, glosses and relations come from Open English WordNet. Dates and parent links come from historical dictionaries, dated corpora and individual citations. Every date and parent link names its source on the sense page. Dates in this prototype are illustrative.

How dates are assigned

A sense is dated by its earliest recorded use. Dates are shown as recorded: an exact date, a year, a decade or a century, such as c. 1300 or 16th c. Rows sort by the earliest possible date.
